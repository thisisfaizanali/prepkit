Senior Backend Engineer, Data Pipelines

Location: Remote (UTC-2 to UTC+3)
Team: Ingestion

About the role
We're looking for a senior backend engineer to join the Ingestion team, which owns the pipeline that receives billions of analytics events every day and makes them queryable within seconds.

What you'll do
- Design, build and operate high-throughput services that ingest and process event data
- Own features end to end, from talking to customers to shipping and monitoring in production
- Improve the reliability and cost-efficiency of our ClickHouse and Kafka infrastructure
- Review code and write design docs that other engineers can build on
- Take part in a shared on-call rotation

Requirements
- 5+ years of professional backend development experience
- Strong proficiency in Python or Go
- Hands-on experience running Kafka (or a similar streaming system) in production
- Solid understanding of SQL and analytical databases
- Experience designing systems for high throughput and low latency
- You have shipped and supported services running on Kubernetes
- Excellent written communication; we are a remote, async-first company
- A track record of mentoring other engineers

Nice to have
- Experience with ClickHouse
- Rust experience
- Contributions to open-source projects
- Familiarity with product analytics tools

What we offer
- Competitive salary and meaningful equity
- Unlimited time off with a 25-day minimum
- A generous home-office budget
- Twice-yearly company offsites

We value diverse perspectives and encourage people from all backgrounds to apply, even if you don't meet every requirement listed.
