About us

Acme Logistics was founded in 2019 in Rotterdam by two former dispatchers who were tired of planning routes in spreadsheets. We are a team of 85 people, 30 of them engineers, and we are backed by a Series B investment.

Our values: we ship small and often, we write things down, and we own what we build — from the first design doc to the pager.
