Joining us

Our interview process has five stages and usually takes three weeks from first call to offer.

  1. Recruiter screen (30 minutes): a conversation about your background and what you are looking for.
  2. Take-home exercise (about 3 hours): build a small service that ingests shipment events and exposes an API. Use any language you like; we care about clarity and tests more than features.
  3. Technical interview (60 minutes): walk us through your take-home, then we extend it together.
  4. System design interview (60 minutes): design a live shipment-tracking system that handles millions of GPS updates a day.
  5. Values conversation with a founder (45 minutes): how you work, how you handle ownership and on-call, and what you want to learn.

We reply to every candidate within five working days after each stage.
